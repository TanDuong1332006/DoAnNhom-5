P199: 4. Problem Statement
P200: What difficulties are users/customers facing?
P201: The online fashion shopping experience still has several difficulties for both customers and merchants. Customers often have difficulty finding products that match their personal preferences, body information, and fashion style because online stores mainly rely on traditional keyword-based search and product categories. In addition, customers may not know whether a product is suitable for their style or needs. 
P202: Merchants also face difficulties in managing products, inventory, customer orders, and understanding changing fashion trends. The lack of personalized recommendations and intelligent search makes the shopping experience less convenient and efficient. 
P203: What is the impact of this issue?
P204: These difficulties can reduce customer satisfaction and make it harder for customers to find suitable products. Customers may spend more time searching, receive irrelevant product recommendations, or purchase products that do not match their preferences. For merchants, inefficient product management and limited customer behavior analysis can make it difficult to understand customer needs and make appropriate business decisions. As the number of products, customers, and orders increases, traditional management methods may also affect the efficiency and scalability of the e-commerce platform.
P205: Why did the team choose to solve this problem?
P206: - The group wants to develop a Smart Online Fashion E-commerce Platform to provide a convenient shopping experience. The platform will provide separate functions for Customers, Merchants, and Administrators to manage products, inventory, orders, payments, and platform activities. Through these functions, the team aims to improve the efficiency of online fashion shopping and support merchants in making better product and sales decisions.

P654: 13. Potential Risks and Mitigation Strategies
P656: Category
P657: Name
P658: Potential risks
P659: Mitigation Strategies
P660: Technologies
P661: System Performance and Scalability
P662: Scalability issues: When the number of customers, merchants, products, and orders increases, the system may experience slow response times or reduced performance.
P663: Design a scalable architecture: Separate major services such as authentication, product management, order management, and payment. Use caching, database indexing, and load testing to improve system performance.
P664: User & Data
P665: User Profile Data
P666: Incorrect or incomplete user information: Customers may provide inaccurate shipping addresses or contact details, which may lead to failed deliveries and order fulfillment issues.
P667: Allow profile updates: Let customers easily edit and verify their profile information and addresses before checkout. Clearly explain that providing accurate information ensures successful order delivery.
P669: Data Privacy
P670: Exposure of personal information: The system processes user information such as accounts, addresses, browsing behavior, and order history. Unauthorized access may result in privacy violations.
P671: Apply privacy protection: Collect only necessary information, use secure data transmission, control access based on user roles, and provide a clear privacy policy explaining how customer data is collected and used.
P672: Security
P673: Authentication and Authorization
P674: Unauthorized account access: Weak authentication or incorrect authorization may allow attackers to access customer, merchant, or administrator accounts.
P675: Implement security best practices: Use secure password hashing, JWT/OAuth 2.0 authentication where appropriate, role-based access control, account validation, session expiration, and protection against common web vulnerabilities.
P677: Online Payment
P678: Payment fraud or transaction failure: Customers may experience failed payments, duplicate transactions, or fraudulent payment activities.
P679: Implement a simulated payment process: Validate simulated transaction status, prevent duplicate transactions, handle failed payment scenarios, and maintain payment and order transaction logs.
P680: Business Operations
P681: Inventory Management
P682: Incorrect stock information: Product inventory may not be updated in real time, causing customers to order products that are already out of stock.
P683: Synchronize inventory: Automatically update stock quantities after successful orders, cancellations, and merchant inventory changes. Validate available quantity again before order confirmation.
P685: Order and Delivery Management
P686: Order or delivery problems: Orders may be delayed, lost, incorrectly updated, or delivered to the wrong address.
P687: Provide order tracking: Maintain clear order statuses such as Pending, Confirmed, Shipping, Delivered, Cancelled, and Returned. Integrate shipping information and allow customers to track order progress.
P688: Merchant Management
P689: Fraudulent or Unqualified Merchants
P690: Low-quality or fraudulent sellers: Unverified merchants may publish misleading products, counterfeit goods, or provide poor services.
P691: Merchant approval process: Require new merchants to provide necessary information and allow administrators to review and approve merchant accounts before they can officially sell products.
P692: Content Management
P693: Inappropriate or Misleading Product Content
P694: Invalid product information: Merchants may upload inappropriate images, misleading descriptions, incorrect prices, or prohibited products.
P695: Content moderation: Allow administrators to review reported or suspicious content, define product publishing rules, and provide mechanisms for customers to report inappropriate products.
P696: Customer Service
P697: Complaints and Disputes
P698: Unresolved customer disputes: Problems related to products, payments, returns, or merchants may negatively affect customer trust.
P699: Complaint management: Provide a complaint and dispute management process where administrators can review evidence, track complaint status, and support appropriate resolutions.
